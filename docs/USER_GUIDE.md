# 🍁 Leafs Ticket Draft — User Guide

*For the ticket partners. Last updated: 2026-11-20.*

Leafs Ticket Draft is our private website for splitting the Maple Leafs season tickets. It replaces the Ticket Tracker spreadsheet: we draft games on a shared board, trade afterwards, and keep every year's results back to 2002-03.

It works on a computer, tablet or phone, and everyone sees picks the moment they happen — so we can draft from wherever we are.

## 1. Getting started

### Create your account
1. Open the site link the commissioner sent you.
2. Tap **Sign up**.
3. Choose a **username**, enter your **display name** (what the others see, e.g. "Rob A"), your **email** (for "you're on the clock" and trade emails, and password resets), a **password** (6+ characters), and the **invite code** from the commissioner.
4. Tap **Create account**. You're logged in and stay logged in on that device for 30 days.

> The first person to sign up becomes the **commissioner** (the one who sets up drafts). Everyone else is a partner.

### Logging in and out
Use your username and password. **Log out** is in the top menu.

### Forgot your password?
On the login screen tap **Forgot your password?**, enter your username or email, and you'll get an email with a reset link (it works once, for 2 hours). If you don't get one — or email isn't set up yet — ask the commissioner; they can send you a reset link by text.

## 2. The Drafts page

This is the home page (**Drafts** in the menu).

- **Current drafts** — drafts being set up or in progress. A live draft shows who is on the clock and for how long.
  - **Enter draft room** opens the board. When it's your turn the button says **Your pick →**.
  - **Preview board** lets you look at a draft that hasn't started yet.
- **Past drafts** — the four most recent finished drafts, with **Board** and **Results** buttons. "All past drafts →" takes you to History.

Tags on each draft: **live / setup / complete**, **Season** or **Playoffs**, **✉ by email** for drafts done over several days, and **imported** for seasons brought in from the old spreadsheet.

## 3. The draft board

### The scoreboard (top)
- **The clock** counts **up** from the moment a partner goes on the clock. After **2:00** it turns **red** and the person on the clock hears a beep — it's a nudge, **there is no time limit**.
- **Round** and **Pick** numbers.
- **On the clock** — who's picking now. It says **YOU'RE ON THE CLOCK** in yellow when it's you, and your browser tab shows "🍁 YOUR PICK".
- **Next up** — the next four picks.
- Small buttons beside the clock: **❚❚ pause** (the commissioner, or you when you're on the clock), **↶ undo** (commissioner), **✉ reminder** (emails whoever is on the clock), **⏱ time out** (anyone in the draft), **⚙ commissioner controls**.

When you go on the clock you'll get a pop-up message and a beep.

### Time outs
Need a minute? **Anyone in the draft** can call a time out with **⏱** (on a phone: **⋯ → Call a time out**).
- The clock stops and **nobody can pick** until it ends. A yellow **⏸ TIME OUT** banner shows everyone who called it and a countdown.
- You can still **look through, search and filter** the board — just not pick.
- It ends when the person who called it taps **▶ Time in**, when a commissioner taps **End time out**, or automatically when the time runs out (**3 minutes** unless the commissioner changed it).
- Then the clock carries on from where it stopped — the time out doesn't count against the pick.

### Who's in the draft room
Under the draft name, each partner has a dot: **green** = has the board open right now, **grey** = away.

### The games
- Each column is a **month** (or a **playoff round**). Each tile is a home game, **in the opponent's colours**, showing the date, start time, price (for the pair of seats), the team's logo and name.
- **Taken** games turn **dark grey** and show who has them (with their colour), the pick number (e.g. **2.3** = round 2, pick 3), **⇄** if the game changed hands in a trade, and **S1/S2** for single seats.
- **PRE** marks preseason games, **PO** playoff games.
- The **ticker** along the bottom shows the latest picks and how long each took.

### Finding games — filters
- **All opponents** — show one team only.
- **Sun … Sat** — tap one or more days (e.g. Fri + Sat).
- **Show** — **Available**, **My games**, **Taken by others**. Turn on any combination; a game shows if it matches **any** of them:
  - *Available + My games* → what's left plus what you already have.
  - *My games + Taken by others* → every game anyone has picked.
- **Search** — type a team, a date ("Nov 7"), a month ("March"), or "round 2 game 3".
- **Clear** resets everything. Your filters are remembered on your device.

## 4. Making a pick

1. When you're on the clock, **tap a game** (it gets a white ring and yellow glow).
2. Tap **Draft game** (or double-click the tile on a computer).
3. Check the confirmation and tap **Lock it in**.

The next partner goes on the clock straight away — even if the clock had been paused. Changed your mind before locking it in? Tap another game, or press **Cancel**. Made a mistake after? Ask the commissioner to **undo**.

**Drafting by email:** some drafts run over a few days. You'll get an email when it's your turn with a link straight to the board. Anyone can tap **✉** to re-send the reminder.

## 5. Your suggested pick and preferences

If you fill in your **Draft preferences** (on your **Profile**), the board shows a line just for you:

> 💡 **Your next pick — suggested:** Sat Nov 7 · Canadiens $404 — *your #1 opponent · Saturday — a day you prefer*

- **Select** jumps to that game and highlights it (a small 💡 also marks the tile).
- **Also:** — two other good options.
- **Why?** — the top three, with every reason for (✓) and against (✗).
- It updates after every pick, so if someone takes your game it moves on.

**Only you can see your preferences and your suggestion** — not the other partners, not even the commissioner. If you don't set any preferences, you won't see a suggestion. It's only advice: you pick whatever you like.

What you can set (all optional) — see [Your profile](#9-your-profile).

## 6. Playoff drafts

Playoff tickets are drafted separately, closer to the playoffs, and **by round and game number** rather than by date.

- Each column is a **round**. With home-ice advantage we host **Games 1, 2, 5 and 7**; without it **Games 3, 4 and 6**. Games 5–7 are marked **if necessary**.
- **Rounds 1–3:** each pick gets **both seats**.
- **Round 4 (the Final):** each pick is **one seat** (tiles say **1 SEAT** and how many seats are left). You can take both seats of a game if both are still open on your turn — or a seat next to a partner.
- We may draft some rounds now (e.g. 1–3) and the rest later (e.g. the Final).
- The playoff order is **drawn at random by the app**. For a later playoff draft the same season, the commissioner can reuse that order or draw a new one; the board notes which.
- Dates and opponents show **TBD** until the commissioner fills them in.

## 7. Trades

Once a draft is finished you can swap games at any time.

1. Go to **Trades**.
2. Pick the **draft** and the partner to **trade with**.
3. Tick the games **you give** and the games **you want** (one side can be empty — e.g. giving a game away). The value of each side is shown.
4. Add a message if you like ("Swap my Tuesday for your Saturday?") and tap **Send request**.

The other partner gets an email and sees a number on their **Trades** menu. They can **Accept** (games change hands immediately) or **Decline**; you can **Withdraw** a request while it's open. Games already played can't be traded. The results pages still show who originally drafted each game.

## 8. History

**History** keeps every draft since **2002-03**.

- **All-time** — each partner's total games, Saturdays and Fri–Sun games, then a table of every season (games and ticket value each). "Order not recorded" means the old spreadsheet only noted who got each game, not the pick sequence.
- **All drafts** — open any draft's **results**:
  - Notes (freebies, spreadsheet notes, how the order was set).
  - The draft order.
  - One panel per partner: games, Fri–Sun games, total value, average and longest pick time, and the list of games with **pick number and ticket cost**.
  - **Results by round** grid (when the pick order is known).
  - **Pick log** — every pick with price, time taken and when it was made (or **Games by date** for older seasons).
  - **Not drafted** — games nobody took.

**Ticket value** is the price for the pair of seats (a single playoff seat counts half), and it includes trades.

## 9. Your profile

**Profile** in the menu.

- **Display name, email, favourite Leaf, board colour** (your colour on the board and history).
- **Email me…** — turn "you're on the clock" and trade emails on or off (password-reset emails always come through).
- **Change password** — enter your current one and a new one.
- **My drafts** — your games, value, average pick time and draft slot for each draft.

### Draft preferences (🔒 only you can see these)
| Preference | Options |
|---|---|
| **Days of the week** | For each day: Prefer / — / Avoid |
| **Favourite opponents** | Up to 5, in order of importance (use ▲▼) |
| **Ticket cost** | Prioritize premium games / No preference |
| **Repeat opponents** | Happy to repeat / No preference / Prefer variety |
| **Preseason games** | Prefer / No preference / Avoid |
| **Start time** | Earlier starts / No preference / Evening (7 PM+) |
| **Spread over the season** | Even out my games per month |
| **Days between my games** | Keep at least N days apart (0 = no preference) |
| **Dates to avoid** | A date or a range, with a label (e.g. "Florida trip") |

Tap **Save preferences**. **Clear all** turns suggestions off.

How much each one counts, roughly: favourite opponents count most; preferred/avoided days next; then premium price, spreading across months, repeats, preseason and start time. Dates you avoid are treated as near deal-breakers.

## 10. Using it on your phone

The site is built to work one-handed on a phone:
- The menu is behind **☰** (top right).
- On the board, the top bar scrolls away; a slim header stays on top with the clock, round/pick, who's on the clock, **next up** and the green/grey **who's here** dots.
- **⋯** opens time out / time in, pause, email reminder, commissioner controls, games and "results so far".
- Use the **search box** or **Filters** (it shows how many filters are on).
- Games are listed **two across**; the month (or round) heading sticks as you scroll.
- Tap a game and a big yellow **Draft** button appears at the bottom, naming the game.

Tip: add the site to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) for one-tap access.

## 11. For the commissioner

### Setting up a season draft
1. **Drafts → + New draft**, Draft type **Season draft**.
2. Check the name and season (it defaults to the next season).
3. **Order:** Snake (normal) or Linear. **How you'll draft:** Live, or By email over a few days. **Clock warning:** minutes before the red warning (0 = none). **Time-out length:** how long a time out can last (default 3 minutes).
4. **Participating partners:** untick anyone sitting out. Any number of partners (2+) works.
5. **Selection order:** last season's order is shown; this year's is pre-filled by rotating it (last pick moves to first). Adjust with ▲▼, **Use rotation** to go back, or **Randomize**.
6. **Schedule:** the official schedule is preselected; or paste/upload a CSV, enter games by hand, or use a sample for testing. Review the games list (remove/add rows).
7. **Create draft**, then **Start draft** when everyone's ready (the clock starts immediately).

### Setting up a playoff draft
**+ New draft → Playoff draft.** For each round: tick it if this draft covers it, set **home ice** (4 or 3 home games), and optionally opponent and price. Rounds already drafted this season are greyed out. Choose to reuse the earlier playoff order or draw a new one. Fill in dates later with **Games…** on the board.

### During a draft (⚙ on the board, or ⋯ on a phone)
- **Pause / resume** the clock (a pick also restarts it).
- **End time out** — ends anyone's time out.
- **Settings** — the **time-out length** (default 3 minutes) and the clock-warning time; change them any time.
- **Restart this pick's clock** — back to 0:00.
- **Undo last pick** — that partner goes back on the clock.
- **Restart draft** — clear all picks and start again at pick 1.
- **Reset to setup** — clear all picks and stop, so you can **edit partners & order**.
- **Draft for someone** — when it's someone else's turn, select a game and tap **Draft for <name>** (the log notes you entered it).
- **Games…** — fill in dates, times, opponents, prices and notes; add a game; remove an unpicked one before the draft starts.

### Partners (menu → Partners)
**More than one commissioner:** use **Make commissioner** to add a backup who can run the draft if you're away — they get all the same controls. There must always be at least one commissioner.

**Activate / Deactivate** (inactive partners can't log in and aren't offered for new drafts; history stays), **Make / Remove commissioner**, **Reset password** (gives you a link to send by text; also emailed if possible), **Delete** (people with history keep their name on past results).

### Importing history (History → Import past drafts)
Match the spreadsheet names (**Q, Abela, Pope**) to accounts and import the seasons converted from the Ticket Tracker workbook; or import a single draft from a CSV (one row per pick: Pick, Partner, Date, Team, …).

## 12. FAQ

**Is there a time limit?** No. The clock turns red after the warning time, but you can take as long as you need.

**Someone called a time out — why can't I pick?** Picks are paused for everyone until the time out ends (the banner shows how long is left). You can still browse and filter.

**I picked the wrong game!** Ask the commissioner to **undo** — you'll be back on the clock with the time you'd already used.

**Can the others see my preferences?** No. Preferences and suggestions are private to you.

**Why is a game grey with "⇄"?** It was traded; the name shown is the current holder.

**What does "order not recorded" mean?** For many older seasons the spreadsheet recorded who got each game but not the order they were picked.

**The logos don't show.** They load from the NHL's website; if one can't load, the tile just shows the name.

**I'm not getting emails.** Check your email on your Profile and that "Email me…" is ticked. If email isn't set up on the server yet, the commissioner can still send reminders and reset links by text.
