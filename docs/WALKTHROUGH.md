# 🍁 Leafs Ticket Draft — Walkthrough Script

*A script for introducing the app to the ticket partners, screen by screen. About 25–30 minutes. Last updated: 2026-11-20.*

**How to use this:** read the **🗣 Say** lines (in your own words), do the **👉 Do** steps on your screen, and pause at **❓ Ask** points. Share your screen on a call, or sit together with a laptop and phones.

---

## Before the session (presenter checklist)
- [ ] The site is running and you know its link (hosted, or a temporary tunnel link).
- [ ] You've changed the **invite code** from the default and have it ready to share.
- [ ] Your commissioner account exists (sign up **first** so you're the commissioner).
- [ ] **History imported:** History → Import past drafts → map Q / Abela / Pope → Import selected. *(Partners must have signed up first — so do this **during** the session, step 2, or ahead of time if they've already joined.)*
- [ ] A **practice draft** created (New draft → season → 2027-28 or a test season, *not started*), so you can start it live.
- [ ] Optional: set some of your own **draft preferences** so the 💡 suggestion appears in the demo.
- [ ] Have a phone handy to show the phone layout.

---

## 1. Why we're doing this (2 min)
🗣 **Say:**
- "For 20-plus years we've run the draft in the Ticket Tracker spreadsheet. This app does the same job, but we can each draft from our own phone or computer, wherever we are, and see every pick instantly."
- "It keeps all our history — every season back to 2002-03 is already in it."
- "Three things are new: trades after the draft, a playoff draft that works by round and game, and an optional private 'suggested pick' for each of us."

---

## 2. Signing up (3 min) — *Login screen*
👉 **Do:** Send the link and the **invite code**. Have each partner tap **Sign up**.

🗣 **Say:**
- "Pick a username and password, and use the display name you want us to see."
- "Please add your **email** — that's how you'll get 'you're on the clock' messages, trade requests, and password resets."
- "You stay logged in on that device for a month. If you ever forget your password, there's a **Forgot your password?** link, or I can send you a reset link."

👉 **Do (commissioner):** once they've joined, go to **History → Import past drafts**, match **Q / Abela / Pope** to their accounts and **Import selected** (if not done already).

❓ **Ask:** "Everyone in? You should see a 'Hi, *name*' page."

---

## 3. The Drafts page (2 min) — *home*
👉 **Do:** Show the **Drafts** page.

🗣 **Say:**
- "This is home. **Current drafts** are at the top — live ones show who's on the clock and for how long. When it's your turn the button says **Your pick →**."
- "Past drafts are underneath; the full list is under **History**."
- "The tags tell you if it's a season or playoff draft, whether it's live or being done **by email** over a few days."

---

## 4. The draft board (6 min) — *Board*
👉 **Do:** Open the practice draft (**Preview board**).

🗣 **Say — the layout:**
- "Every home game is a tile, **in the other team's colours**, in columns by month. Each tile shows the date, time, the price for our pair of seats, and the team."
- "Up top is the **clock** — it counts **up** from when you go on the clock. After two minutes it turns red and beeps at you, but **there's no time limit**."
- "**Round** and **Pick**, who's **on the clock**, and who's **next up**."
- "Under the title you can see **who's in the draft room** — green dot means they have the board open."

🗣 **Say — finding games:**
- "Filter by **opponent**, or tap **days** — say Friday and Saturday."
- "The **Show** buttons combine: *Available + My games* shows what's left plus what you've got; *My games + Taken by others* shows everything that's been picked."
- "Or just **search** — a team, 'Nov 7', 'March'."

👉 **Do:** Tap **Fri** and **Sat**, then **Available**; then **Clear**.

❓ **Ask:** "Can everyone see the board on their own screen?"

---

## 5. A practice pick (5 min) — *Board, live*
👉 **Do:** Click **Start draft** on the board. The first partner goes on the clock.

🗣 **Say:**
- "When you're on the clock it says **YOU'RE ON THE CLOCK**, you'll hear a beep, and your browser tab says 'YOUR PICK'."
- "**Tap a game**, tap **Draft game**, check it, and **Lock it in**. The next person goes on the clock instantly — watch everyone's screen update."
- "Taken games turn grey with the person's name and the pick number — '2.3' is round 2, pick 3."
- "The ticker at the bottom shows recent picks and how long each one took."

👉 **Do:** Let each partner make one pick. Wait past 2:00 once to show the red warning.

🗣 **Say — mistakes and pauses:**
- "If you pick the wrong one, I can **undo** it and you're back on the clock."
- "Need a break? I can **pause** the clock — or you can, when it's your turn. As soon as someone locks in a pick, the clock starts again for the next person."
- "If someone's away, I can **draft on their behalf** — the log will show I entered it."

🗣 **Say — time outs:**
- "Anyone in the draft can call a **time out** with ⏱ — on a phone it's under ⋯. The clock stops and nobody can pick, but you can all keep browsing and filtering."
- "Everyone sees the yellow banner with a countdown. The person who called it taps **Time in**, or I can end it — otherwise it ends by itself after **3 minutes**. I can change that length."

👉 **Do:** Have a partner call a time out; point out the banner on everyone's screen and that tiles can't be picked; have them tap **Time in**.

---

## 6. Your private suggested pick (3 min) — *Profile → Draft preferences, then Board*
👉 **Do:** Open **Profile**, scroll to **Draft preferences**.

🗣 **Say:**
- "This is optional and **completely private** — no one else can see your preferences or your suggestions, me included."
- "You can mark days you **prefer** or **avoid**, rank up to **five favourite opponents**, choose to **prioritize premium games**, say whether you like **repeat opponents** or prefer **variety**, avoid **preseason**, prefer **earlier or evening** starts, **even out your games by month**, keep a few **days between games**, and block out **dates you're away**."
- "Save it, and on the board you'll see **💡 your suggested pick** with the reasons. **Select** jumps to it; **Why?** shows the top three and every reason. It's just advice."

👉 **Do:** Back on the board, show the 💡 line, **Why?**, then **Select**.

❓ **Ask:** "Anyone want to set theirs up now?"

---

## 7. Drafting on the phone (2 min) — *Board on a phone*
👉 **Do:** Show the board on a phone.

🗣 **Say:**
- "On a phone the menu is under **☰**. The board keeps a slim header with the clock and who's up; **⋯** has pause and the other actions."
- "Use **search** or **Filters**. Games are two across, with the month stuck at the top as you scroll."
- "Tap a game and a big yellow **Draft** button appears at the bottom with the game's name — right under your thumb."
- "Tip: add it to your home screen."

---

## 8. Playoff drafts (2 min) — *New draft → Playoff* (commissioner view)
👉 **Do:** Open **+ New draft**, switch **Draft type** to **Playoff draft** (don't create it).

🗣 **Say:**
- "Playoffs are drafted separately, by **round and game number**. With home ice we host games 1, 2, 5 and 7; without it 3, 4 and 6 — I set that per round."
- "We can draft some rounds now and the rest later — for example rounds 1 to 3, then the Final."
- "Rounds 1 to 3 are picked as **pairs**. In the **Final** each pick is **one seat**, so two of us can go together — or you can take both seats if they're both still open on your turn."
- "The order is drawn at **random** by the app. For a later playoff draft I can reuse that order or draw a new one."

---

## 9. Trades (2 min) — *Trades*
👉 **Do:** Open **Trades** (use a finished draft, e.g. the imported 2026-27).

🗣 **Say:**
- "After a draft you can trade any time. Pick the partner, tick what you'd **give** and what you'd **get** — you'll see the value of each side — add a note and send."
- "They get an email and a number on their Trades menu, and can **accept** or **decline**. Accepted games switch owners right away; the history still shows who drafted them."
- "Games that have already been played can't be traded."

---

## 10. History (2 min) — *History*
👉 **Do:** Open **History**, then the **2026-27** results, then an older season (e.g. **2002-03**).

🗣 **Say:**
- "**All-time** totals for each of us — games, Saturdays, Friday-to-Sunday games — and every season's games and value."
- "Open any draft: the order, each person's games with **pick number and ticket cost**, the round-by-round grid, and the full pick log with times."
- "For many older seasons the spreadsheet didn't record the pick order, so those show games by date. 2002-03 did — that was the 3/8-shares year."
- "Ticket value matches the spreadsheet; I also fixed a few spreadsheet quirks during the import."

---

## 11. Profile and housekeeping (1 min) — *Profile*
🗣 **Say:**
- "On your **Profile**: display name, email, whether you get emails, your **board colour**, and changing your password."
- "**My drafts** summarizes your picks and value for every draft."
- *(Commissioner)* "Under **Partners** I can add a **backup commissioner** who can run the draft if I'm away — there's always at least one."

---

## 12. Wrap-up and questions (2 min)
🗣 **Say:**
- "Recap: sign up, check your profile and email, set preferences if you want suggestions, and you're ready."
- "For the real draft I'll set it up, and when everyone's in the room — you'll see the green dots — I'll start it."
- "The **User Guide** has all of this written down."

❓ **Ask:**
- "Live together, or by email over a few days for the next draft?"
- "Any preferences or features you'd like changed?"

👉 **Do (after):** **Reset to setup** or delete the practice draft (⚙ → Reset to setup, or Delete on the Drafts page) so it doesn't clutter history.
